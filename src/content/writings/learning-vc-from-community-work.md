title: What community organizing taught me over the years
date: 2025-07-08
---

I started building and helping communities when i was 17, initially with some curiosity while scrolling past instagram and came across the founder promoting the cause for mental health for teenagers "teenvisor" which i felt more interesting and wanted to support the cause and eventually reached out to the founder and got in to support the community and its vision , teenvisor supported all the major societal causes globally and addressed its issues and at the same it helped the people who seeked help , together we all stood strong for the mission to prevent and nurture the phenomenon of emotional and psychological well being of teenagers


This phase shaped my pov on communities and its impact on young folks understanding what disruption it could cause at an early age while researching and working on this community, this navigated me to variabl many opportunities around community volunteering and building together ,my next work was with foniglobal.com an NGO where I designed works on the voice on the youth , it gave me a global community to connect with followed with saarthi health which focuses on the mental wellbeing on the young teenagers whee we hosted a workshop and a meetup to connect indivisuals together in the journey of mental stability assisted by the psychologists which largely helped folks around 

Then one day i decided to go to a hackathon and this changed the entire idea of networking to me , got to meet a guy who was eventually volunteering at the hackathon and this person was equally driven by communities which came at the right time and  one day we decided to build a community of our own "CodeonJVM Chennai" a community that focuses on the JVM tools and technologies , the vision was to  to broaden the opportunities to everyone like ours through building and learning from communities

But the real picture came with hosting events in real , taking up this job was way more exciting and exhilarating, from rolling out registrations and finding our potential sponsors for events and finding a space to organise and managing the audiences, these were exactly that gave the hinderences in community builidng, we started as a community of 4 people to community of 6k+ individuals ,  a small group which started in whatsapp to a growing community in all socials 

we have hosted 25+ events for the past two years including hackathons , workshops and speaker sessions, communities arent just a space to learn but to give it back the same
